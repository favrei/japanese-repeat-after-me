# Art & voice brief — missing-wallet 消えた財布

Requirements only; no art or audio is approved. Derived from [`story.md`](story.md).

## Required keys

- `scenes.store-register`, `scenes.store-register-close`
- `scenes.koban-night`, `scenes.koban-evening`
- `characters.store-clerk` — 店員
- `characters.police-officer` — 警察官

The two store keys should be crops of one plate. The two kōban keys should reuse
one interior plate with night/evening light variation, not create unrelated
rooms.

## Scenes

- **Store:** station-area convenience store at 10 p.m., medium register view;
  basket, phone, and queue readable without real branding. Mood is pressured
  but ordinary. Keep the register total and wallet identification in UI, not
  generated signage.
- **Kōban:** small neighborhood police box counter, first at night and then the
  following evening. Form, wallet, sticker, card, and fictional ID are visible
  props. Mood is procedural safety, never crime drama.
- Standard full-bleed seinen system: newsprint, neutral ink, one UI-owned deep
  red accent, hard edges, safe upper balloon zone, no chibi.

## Characters

### Store clerk

- **Persona:** woman, early 20s; light clear mid-range voice, brisk.
- **Character:** efficient without being cold.

| Trait | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Efficient | One hand controls the held basket while the other points to the exit route; squared working posture. |
| Not cold | Direct eye contact for one beat, restrained concern rather than a smile. |

No exaggerated sympathy, suspicion, or curiosity. States: neutral register
attention, directional gesture, restrained concern.

### Police officer

- **Persona:** man, mid-30s; neutral low-mid voice, patient and precise.
- **Character:** procedural and quietly reassuring.

| Trait | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Procedural | Form and pen aligned, wallet handled carefully, posture square to the counter. |
| Quietly reassuring | Calm attention and open hands; no dramatic detective pose. |

No flashing lights, interrogation imagery, blame, or triumph. States: neutral
intake, evidence-checking focus, small completion nod.

The learner is not drawn.

## Cover

A small black wallet under the edge of a convenience-store receipt, with the
kōban light visible beyond glass. The red cat sticker may be the cover's single
story accent only if the pack contract permits; otherwise keep it neutral and
leave red to UI.
