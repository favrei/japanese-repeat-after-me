# Art & voice brief — moving-day 引っ越しの日

Requirements only; no art or audio is approved. Derived from [`story.md`](story.md).

## Required keys

- `scenes.apartment-morning`, `scenes.hallway-sofa`
- `scenes.kitchen-evening`, `scenes.hallway-night`
- `characters.mover` — 引っ越しスタッフ
- `characters.gas-technician` — 点検スタッフ
- `characters.neighbor` — 隣人

The morning apartment and sofa hallway may share one plate with a push-in. The
night hallway must visibly be the same building. Full-bleed adult seinen frame,
neutral ink, UI-owned deep red, safe balloon/control zones, no real company
logos.

## Scenes

- **Apartment morning:** empty new room, marked boxes, desk, window, and moving
  blankets. Mood: purposeful disorder.
- **Hallway sofa:** elevator opening and oversized sofa read immediately. Avoid
  comic slapstick; the problem is dimensional, not a gag.
- **Kitchen evening:** compact water heater and control, technician toolbox,
  first settled light in the apartment.
- **Hallway night:** boxes now inside, two neighboring doors, quiet shared
  corridor. Mood: cautious beginning of belonging.

## Characters

### Mover

- **Persona:** man, early 30s; energetic clear mid-range voice.
- **Character:** physically brisk and detail-first.

| Trait | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Brisk | Weight already forward, gloves on, hands always on the next object. |
| Detail-first | Eyes on labels and clearance; protective blanket prepared before contact. |

No clowning with the sofa, judgment of possessions, or careless pose.

### Gas technician

- **Persona:** woman, early 40s; firm neutral mid-range voice, evenly paced.
- **Character:** safety-minded and literal.

| Trait | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Safety-minded | Tools ordered, control checked before demonstration, stable stance. |
| Literal | Points exactly to the named knob; expression changes only when the reading changes. |

No alarmism, domestic assumptions, or friendly improvisation.

### Neighbor

- **Persona:** woman, early 60s; dry warm low-mid voice, unhurried.
- **Character:** direct about rules and practical about help.

| Trait | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Direct | Square posture in her doorway, no coy angle or passive-aggressive smile. |
| Practical | One hand indicates the manager's floor; ordinary indoor clothes, ready to return home. |

No gossip, gift ceremony, scowl, or exaggerated welcome.

The learner is never drawn.

## Cover

One red-marked box in an otherwise empty room, with the neighboring door just
visible down the hall. The image promises both work and a new social boundary.
