# Art & voice brief — rain-on-mountain 山で雨

Requirements only; no art or audio is approved. Derived from [`story.md`](story.md).

## Required keys

- `scenes.trailhead`, `scenes.darkening-trail`, `scenes.mountain-shelter`,
  `scenes.lower-trail`
- `characters.hiking-partner` — 同行者
- `characters.shelter-attendant` — 小屋の係員

Use three coherent source environments: trailhead/lower-trail continuity,
upper trail under darkening cloud, and sturdy enclosed shelter. No identifiable
real mountain, unsafe shortcut, tree-as-shelter, or heroic summit imagery.

## Scenes

- **Trailhead:** map and information board under bright morning cloud; rain gear
  visible but not worn. Mood: deliberate preparation.
- **Darkening trail:** below a ridge rather than on a summit, western cloud
  mass visible, path still readable. Mood: evidence arriving, not disaster.
- **Shelter:** substantial enclosed hut below ridge, radar/map visible inside;
  rain and thunder remain outside.
- **Lower trail:** wet marked route, pair-sized path, trailhead sign in distance.
  Slipperiness reads through texture, not a fall.
- Adult restrained seinen style, full-bleed, neutral ink and screentone, UI-owned
  deep red, safe overlay zones.

## Characters

### Hiking partner

- **Persona:** woman, early 30s; clear low-mid voice, calm and outdoorsy.
- **Character:** cautious without fear, decisive without heroics.

| Trait | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Cautious | Map checked before movement, weather app visible, practical layered clothing. |
| Decisive | Body turns downhill as soon as evidence changes; no backward summit gaze. |

States: planning focus, weather recognition, controlled descent. No fear pose,
bravado, or glamorous mountaineer styling.

### Shelter attendant

- **Persona:** man, late 50s; rough quiet low voice, slow and exact.
- **Character:** locally factual and unsentimental.

| Trait | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Locally factual | Finger on marked east route and eyes on radar, not on the hikers' reactions. |
| Unsentimental | Weathered practical layers, still posture, no rescue-hero staging. |

States: neutral watch, route explanation, quiet release. The learner is not drawn.

## Cover

A folded map held against the first drops of rain, with the summit obscured and
the lower shelter roof visible. The cover should endorse turning back, not sell
the danger.
