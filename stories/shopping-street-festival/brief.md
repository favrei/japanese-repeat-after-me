# Art & voice brief — shopping-street-festival 商店街の夏祭り

Requirements only; no art or audio is approved. Derived from [`story.md`](story.md).

## Required keys

- `scenes.festival-stall-open`, `scenes.festival-stall-shelf`,
  `scenes.festival-stall-customer`, `scenes.festival-stall-rain`
- `characters.stall-leader` — 係員
- `characters.festival-visitor` — お客さん

All scenes should reuse one shopping-street/stall plate with crop, crowd, light,
and rain variations. Lettering may show generic price/rules, but the UI remains
the authoritative text.

## Scenes

- Covered shopping street at summer dusk, ring-toss stall, lanterns, visible
  payment box, throwing line, prize shelf, and distant warehouse door.
- Customer state adds a short orderly line and one child silhouette without
  making a child the speaking character.
- Rain state is fine rain under cover, paper goods being protected, no storm.
- Adult full-bleed seinen slice-of-life, restrained crowd detail, neutral ink,
  UI-owned deep red, no carnival/kawaii exaggeration.

## Characters

| Cast | Persona | Character | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stall leader | man, early 50s; strong warm mid-low | community-practical, momentum-driven | Sleeves up, clipboard and stock box already moving; points once, never looms. |
| Visitor | woman, mid-30s; bright neutral mid-range | task-focused, unembarrassed to ask | Stands square to rules board with child beside her; questions read as practical, not confused. |

States: leader neutral assignment/restock/rain closure; visitor neutral question,
boundary check, prize-location check. Learner is not drawn.

## Cover

Ring-toss hoops, lanterns, and a prize shelf just before opening, with one empty
volunteer chair in first person.
