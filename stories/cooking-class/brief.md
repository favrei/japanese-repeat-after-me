# Art & voice brief — cooking-class 二人の料理教室

Requirements only; no art or audio is approved. Derived from [`story.md`](story.md).

## Required keys

- `scenes.class-counter`, `scenes.class-cutting`, `scenes.class-stove`,
  `scenes.class-plating`
- `characters.cooking-partner` — パートナー

All four scene keys should be crops of one teaching-kitchen plate with changing
counter props. Keep knives and heat ordinary and controlled, never dramatic.

## Scenes

- Evening adult cooking class, paired stainless or wood worktop, visible recipe,
  vegetables, pot, plating/photo station.
- Progress is shown through ingredients → cut vegetables → simmering pot →
  plated curry, not through costume or camera jumps.
- Full-bleed restrained seinen style, neutral ink and screentone, UI-owned deep
  red, safe overlay zones, no kawaii food mascots or glossy food advertisement.

## Character

- **Persona:** man, early 30s; warm light mid-range voice, relaxed.
- **Character:** collaborative and recipe-faithful.

| Trait | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Collaborative | Occupies half the station, leaves tools centered for sharing, looks toward the work rather than supervising learner. |
| Recipe-faithful | Finger follows recipe line before each new step; no chef pose or improvisational flourish. |

States: relaxed task division, focused prep, small satisfaction at plating. No
teacher stance, chef costume, correction face, or romantic framing. Learner not
drawn.

## Cover

Two sets of tools facing one shared pile of ingredients, with the recipe card
between them.
