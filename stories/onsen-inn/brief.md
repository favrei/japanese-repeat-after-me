# Art & voice brief — onsen-inn 温泉旅館の一泊

Requirements only; no art or audio is approved. Derived from [`story.md`](story.md).

## Required keys

- `scenes.ryokan-entrance`, `scenes.tatami-room`, `scenes.bath-entrance`,
  `scenes.ryokan-morning`
- `characters.ryokan-attendant` — 旅館スタッフ
- `characters.bath-attendant` — 温泉の係員

Entrance evening/morning should reuse one plate with light variation. The bath
scene stops outside the changing/bathing area: no nudity, bodies, or privacy
risk. Generic facility lettering only.

## Scenes

- **Ryokan entrance:** mountain inn at dusk, shoe area and desk visible; morning
  version adds luggage and shuttle timetable.
- **Tatami room:** yukata sizes, towel, room safe, low table; no tourist-kitsch
  overload.
- **Bath entrance:** corridor, noren, rules card, lockers; quiet and private.
- Adult full-bleed seinen treatment, restrained neutral ink, UI-owned red, safe
  overlay zones; no luxury advertisement or mystical steam imagery.
