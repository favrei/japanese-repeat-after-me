# Art & voice brief — neighborhood-clinic 町のクリニック

Requirements only; no art or audio is approved. Derived from [`story.md`](story.md).

## Required keys

- `scenes.clinic-reception`, `scenes.clinic-intake`, `scenes.exam-room`,
  `scenes.pharmacy-counter`
- `characters.receptionist` — 受付
- `characters.nurse` — 看護師
- `characters.doctor` — 医師
- `characters.pharmacist` — 薬剤師

Reception and intake may share one clinic plate with a push-in. The pharmacy is
a distinct establishment. Use fictional forms and labels; no real patient data,
drug brand, hospital logo, diagnosis text, or dosage in generated art.

## Scenes

- **Reception/intake:** modest neighborhood clinic, morning, form racks and
  waiting numbers visible. Mood: orderly access, not sterile intimidation.
- **Exam room:** desk, throat light, chart, no invasive imagery. Mood: factual
  concentration.
- **Pharmacy:** evening counter with sealed medicine bag and instruction sheet.
  Mood: deliberate closure.
- Standard full-bleed adult seinen frame, neutral ink, UI-owned deep red, quiet
  overlay zones, no childlike medical mascots.

## Characters

| Cast | Persona | Character | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Receptionist | woman, late 30s; clear neutral mid-range | administratively exact, solution-first | Forms already sorted; one direct document gesture; no medical performance. |
| Nurse | man, late 20s; light low-mid, patient | attentive, systematic | Thermometer and form handled in a fixed sequence; open listening posture. |
| Doctor | woman, early 50s; low steady, concise | evidence-bound, spare | Eyes on visible finding and chart; minimal gesture; no reassuring smile before evidence. |
| Pharmacist | man, early 40s; warm low-mid, deliberate | repetition-friendly, safety-first | Label and medicine shown together; finger tracks frequency and condition. |

Each needs neutral, focused, and small completion states. No role may look like
another generic white-coat figure; posture and props must express the declared
agenda. The learner is never drawn.

## Cover

A questionnaire, thermometer, and sealed medicine bag arranged across one
quiet clinic counter. No symptom is sensationalized.
