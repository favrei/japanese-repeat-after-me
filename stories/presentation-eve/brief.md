# Art & voice brief — presentation-eve プレゼン前夜

Requirements only; no art or audio is approved. Derived from [`story.md`](story.md).

## Required keys

- `scenes.office-screen-wide`, `scenes.office-screen-chart`,
  `scenes.office-rehearsal`, `scenes.client-meeting`
- `characters.presentation-coworker` — 同僚
- `characters.client-manager` — 顧客

Night-office stages should use one plate with monitor crop and later clock/notes
state. Client meeting is a second plate using the same fictional deck. Generated
charts may contain simple abstract marks, but authoritative numbers/text stay in
UI; no real company, product, or client identity.

## Scenes

- **Night office:** small meeting room after normal hours, unfinished slides,
  work-time chart, clock passing nine, notes becoming cleaner across stages.
- **Client meeting:** morning, more formal room, same deck and a visible written
  contract-note page; restrained tension, no courtroom staging.
- Adult full-bleed seinen register, neutral ink and screentone, UI-owned deep
  red, safe overlays, no glossy corporate-stock aesthetic.

## Characters

### Coworker

- **Persona:** woman, early 30s; crisp mid-low voice, quick and controlled.
- **Character:** analytical and unsentimental about slides.

| Trait | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Analytical | Finger on timing/data label, notes grouped by status rather than color decoration. |
| Unsentimental | Deletes or moves a slide without defensive expression; body oriented to screen, not approval. |

### Client manager

- **Persona:** man, late 50s; deep neutral voice, deliberate and concise.
- **Character:** skeptical but fair.

| Trait | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Skeptical | Contract page open, eyes on consequence line, no performative frown. |
| Fair | Gives the two-month scenario plainly and waits; open hand requests written evidence. |

States: coworker neutral analysis/data caution/rehearsal focus; client neutral
question/scenario/written-request. Learner never drawn.

## Cover

An unfinished deck glowing in an otherwise dark meeting room, with a ten-minute
timer and three handwritten priorities in the foreground.
