# Art & voice brief — secondhand-bicycle 中古の自転車

Requirements only; no art or audio is approved. Derived from [`story.md`](story.md).

## Required keys

- `scenes.bike-shop-front`, `scenes.test-ride-area`, `scenes.bike-workbench`,
  `scenes.bike-shop-door`
- `characters.bike-shop-owner` — 店主

All four scenes belong to one small used-bicycle shop and should derive from at
most two source plates. Show generic tags and a fictional registration sticker;
no real business, police, or association marks.

## Scenes

- **Front/ride:** two visibly different commuter bicycles, basket and frame
  weight difference legible through shape, a marked safe test lane.
- **Workbench:** brake cable, saddle lever, protective tools; never imply that
  the learner repairs the bicycle without staff.
- **Door/handover:** same serviced blue bicycle, neutral registration label and
  card visible, late-afternoon continuity.
- Adult full-bleed seinen treatment; shop detail in middle band, safe balloons
  above, controls below, one UI-owned deep-red accent.

## Character

- **Persona:** man, late 40s; dry clear mid-low voice, moderately paced.
- **Character:** mechanically candid and anti-salesy.

| Trait | Visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Mechanically candid | Worn clean hands, eyes on components, points to the brake rather than the price tag. |
| Anti-salesy | Neutral stance beside both options, no presenting flourish, no broad smile after the choice. |

States: neutral comparison, focused inspection, small handover nod. No greasy
caricature, cycling-racer styling, hype, or hidden defect. The learner is not
drawn.

## Cover

Two used bicycles outside a narrow shop, one basket-heavy and one lighter blue
frame, with the workbench visible inside.
