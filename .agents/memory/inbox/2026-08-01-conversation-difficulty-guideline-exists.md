# Conversation difficulty guideline exists

The project now has a durable authoring and assessment document at
`.agents/documents/japanese-conversation-difficulty-guideline.md`.

Use it whenever a conversation is requested or assessed at JLPT-anchored
`N5`, `N4`, `N3`, `N2`, or `N1`. It contains situation-neutral level profiles,
varied calibration examples, separate listening and learner-production
ratings, boundary labels, and a repeatable five-dimension difficulty test.

The guideline is also linked from `.agents/documents/INDEX.md` and Step 5 of
`.agents/documents/stage-design-flow.md`.
