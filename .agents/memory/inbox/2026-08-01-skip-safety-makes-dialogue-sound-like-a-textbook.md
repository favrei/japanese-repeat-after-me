# Skip-safety is why authored dialogue sounds like a textbook

2026-08-01, found while drafting office-gig. The user read the first draft and
said: "it looks ok, but the conversation is awkward... please make the
conversation more natural." The draft had passed every gate.

**The cause is structural, not carelessness.** Real Japanese conversation runs
on tokens that acknowledge what the other person just said — そうなんですね、
わかります、本当ですか、なるほど. Skip-safety bans all of them, because the
learner's turn is a bubble that Skip can erase, so any line reacting to it
dangles in a pessimistic run. Remove them and nothing replaces them: what is
left is a row of clean standalone declaratives that satisfy every check and
sound like a textbook.

「わかります。」 was the most natural possible opener for one office-gig line and
was unusable for exactly this reason. That is a permanent tax on every story in
this app, not a one-off.

The recovery techniques, now written into Step 4 of
`.agents/documents/stage-design-flow.md` as "Naturalness under skip-safety":

- **Sentence-final particles instead of reactions.** よね reaches for agreement
  without claiming any was given. Highest-value substitution by far.
- **Opinions instead of facts.** 「私はいつもギターの音で選びます。」 (fact
  recited at nobody) → 「ギターがいいと、それだけで聞いちゃいますよね。」 Same
  information, same skip-safety, but a person says the second one.
- **Colloquial register for the other party only.** Contractions and casual
  shapes make one speaker sound spoken — and since the learner never contracts,
  this *strengthens* Pass C blind attribution. Naturalness and separation
  reinforce each other rather than competing, which was not obvious beforehand.
- **Let the learner use 〜んです.** 好きです → 好きなんです. Beginner-safe, and
  it buys a lot for nothing.
- **〜て rather than 〜ので** to link a reason: ので is the written form, and the
  spoken form is usually shorter too (one bubble went 24 → 20 morae).
- **Specifics over generic nouns.** Also catches dangling referents — 「そのバンド」
  pointed at a band the script never names, which was stiff *and* broken.

Do not resolve the tension by weakening skip-safety.
