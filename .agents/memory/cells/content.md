# Content

Canonical memory for future chapter creation, private preview, review,
publication, and canonical reference audio.

## Phase boundary

- Accounts and chapter publishing are a confirmed future direction, not
  current implementation scope.
- The current product may begin with locally supplied or bundled conversation
  material. Do not let future multi-user publishing machinery complicate the
  first rehearsal experience.

## Account and chapter direction

- Add an account system using Google identity.
- An authenticated user may upload a new conversation chapter.
- A new chapter remains private to its uploader, who may immediately play and
  test it.
- Release to other users requires review by both the project owner and an
  agent.

Minimal lifecycle:

```text
private draft → review → released
```

The private draft remains usable by its uploader while review is pending.

Google authentication establishes identity. Access to Gmail mailbox data is
not required by the current direction.

## Chapter audio policy

- Require text for every dialogue bubble.
- Generate text-to-speech on demand for immediate private preview.
- Let the uploader optionally replace generated speech with human audio.
- Store audio per bubble or turn, not as one whole-conversation recording, so
  playback can pause, repeat, and branch with the rehearsal flow.
- During review, either approve the uploaded audio or generate and freeze a
  canonical audio asset for the bubble.
- Released chapters use reviewed, versioned audio assets and cache them for
  reliable playback and offline use.
- On-the-fly synthesis is a private-preview or fallback mechanism, not the
  canonical audio generated anew for every released playback. This avoids
  voice and output differences across browsers and devices.
