# Visual Design

Canonical memory for the persistent story frame, visual register, responsive
layout, swappable art slots, and pending asset work.

## Current direction

- Preserve the user-confirmed dialogue and bubble progression independently of
  visual work. The frame may change, but flow logic, state handling, and test
  selectors must not change by implication. See [Product](product.md).
- The café is one example story, not the product identity. The constant UI
  frame surrounds swappable scene, character, and cover art; later chapter
  authors may supply story-specific assets.
- The user initially selected a manga-storybook frame on 2026-07-30. That frame
  was implemented in `poc/`, but the user then rejected its childlike register
  and reported that its centered `680px` column was not full screen on desktop.
- The approved replacement direction is **seinen slice-of-life manga** with a
  **full-bleed responsive desktop scene**. This supersedes the earlier kawaii
  styling while retaining the layer model and the café-as-example principle.
- `.agents/resources/seinen-manga-frame/mock.html` is the working design
  reference for the replacement. The user approved the direction and design
  system, not every concrete detail in the mockup. Its known defects must not
  be copied blindly.
- The replacement has not been ported into `poc/`. The currently modified PoC
  source still contains the rejected childlike manga frame and placeholder art.

## Constant frame and swappable layers

The persistent composition has three layers:

1. a swappable story background;
2. a swappable other-party character sprite, with the learner represented in
   first person and no learner sprite;
3. constant UI chrome for exit/skip controls, progress, one speech balloon,
   action controls, and feedback.

Speaker identity and balloon-tail direction remain part of the constant frame.
Story-specific art must not contain dialogue text because text-to-image
generation is unreliable for lettering and product text belongs in the UI.

## Approved visual system

- **Register:** adult, restrained seinen slice-of-life rather than chibi,
  kawaii, shonen, or shojo.
- **Typography:** Shippori Mincho B1 for dialogue and narration; Zen Kaku Gothic
  New for UI and Latin text. Both are Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font
  License. Self-host Japanese subsets for offline PWA delivery rather than
  making the CDN a runtime requirement.
- **Palette:** newsprint paper `#efece4`, true/dark ink around `#14120f`,
  neutral ink greys, and exactly one deep manga-red accent around `#a81c22`.
  Do not carry forward the former yellow, pink, or blue accents.
- **Ink and tone:** thin, confident, varied line weight; black/white value
  structure; 45-degree dot screentone around 20–40% for mid-values; no soft
  shadows, gradients, cel shading, or thick cartoon outlines.
- **Geometry:** hard-edged rectangular panels and tight elliptical balloons
  with sharp triangular tails.
- **Characters:** realistic adult proportions around six to seven heads tall,
  restrained expressions, and no dot eyes, blush circles, or chibi
  deformation.
- **Responsive layout:** scene art fills the entire viewport. Balloons and
  controls overlay it within readable width constraints. Do not restore a
  centered fixed-width application column with bare desktop gutters.

The mockup records implementation-ready tokens and a CSS screentone technique,
but it is design evidence rather than production code.

## Rejected implementation and audit evidence

The uncommitted frame currently in `poc/` uses Hachi Maru Pop, Klee One, a warm
paper/yellow/pink/blue palette, rounded controls, thick outlines, a chibi SVG
staff character, and `width: min(100%, 680px)` containers. The user rejected
that tone as looking intended for a very young child.

Its visual audit also found:

- the foreground counter consumes too much space as a flat slab;
- the balloon, character, AUTO chip, sound-effect text, and lamps collide in
  some states;
- emoji icons and an explicit dashed placeholder box read as unfinished;
- the scene lacks visual anchors and meaningful motion;
- approximate kana marks can read as “all wrong” when misses dominate;
- wide desktop viewports expose empty gutters and stop short of the viewport
  bottom.

The replacement mockup still has unresolved problems:

- its placeholder character reads as a robed figure, floats visually, and
  collides with the balloon tail;
- its failure treatment is too loud and shonen-like for the selected register;
- its black counter band remains a dead slab;
- placeholder scene geometry is too sparse on wide screens;
- the failure caption wraps poorly at `412px`.

## Pending art assets

All generated scene art should be monochrome seinen ink on newsprint, contain
no text or letters, and reserve the red accent for UI rather than the art.

- `poc/public/assets/scene-cafe.png`: approximately `1600×1200`, composed to
  tolerate full-bleed cropping. Keep the upper-middle quiet for balloon
  overlays and the lower-left simple for the character; use meaningful café
  objects rather than a flat black lower band.
- `poc/public/assets/char-staff-{neutral,happy,sad}.png`: transparent PNGs,
  approximately `600×880`, showing the same realistically proportioned adult
  café worker with restrained expression changes.
- `poc/public/assets/cover-cafe.png`: approximately `1100×690` landscape cover
  art for the story entry panel.
- `poc/public/icon-192.png` and `poc/public/icon-512.png`: a monochrome ink app
  mark on newsprint, replacing scaffold placeholders.

If an agent lacks text-to-image capability, keep the existing placeholders and
leave one precise inbox note. Do not substitute unrelated stock art or
known-wrong shipped assets.

## Implementation and QA history

- The rejected manga-storybook port changed `poc/app/globals.css`,
  `PracticeApp.tsx`, layout metadata, stage titles, manifest, service worker,
  tests, and README while preserving gameplay.
- It introduced mood-specific placeholder character states and approximate
  target-reading hit/miss marks. The feedback marks are transcript-string
  alignment, not acoustic phoneme evidence. See [Recognition](recognition.md).
- Its last recorded verification passed type checking, lint, build, and
  `13/13` tests, plus a `412×915` Chrome click-through covering staff autoplay,
  speaking, near miss, failure, success, and completion.
- This mechanical pass does not approve the rejected visual treatment or the
  replacement mockup.
