# Ten-story expansion

Status: **authoring complete; production media and app implementation not started.**

The user selected this slate and authorized the full authoring pass on
2026-08-01. All ten standalone story packs now include the script, character
cards, readings, accepted variants, difficulty verdict, pessimistic all-skip
proof, five-pass character-separation verdict, art/voice brief, and a voice
manifest. See the [`../../stories/AUTHORING-REPORT.md`](../../stories/AUTHORING-REPORT.md)
for the detached validation record.

This plan expands the library with ten **standalone stories**. Each story keeps
the established authoring scale of 2–4 stages; the request is not for one
ten-stage continuation or for ten stages inside every story.

## Selection criteria

- sentences a learner is reasonably likely to need in Japan;
- a concrete result at the end, not only a topic;
- enough real goal-state changes for a short story without padding;
- variety across travel, home, health, social, outdoor, and professional life;
- a manageable number of scenes and recurring characters;
- a progression from N4 core material into one advanced professional story.

## Deliberately deferred

- `Stranded by a Typhoon` overlaps the transport disruption of *Missed the
  Last Train* and the weather decisions of *Rain on the Mountain*.
- `The Leaking Ceiling` and `The Missing Delivery` are useful future resident
  stories, but *Moving Day* covers more reusable housing language first.
- `Joining a Photography Club` remains the strongest social-story alternate;
  *Cooking Class* currently wins on immediate spoken actions and production
  economy.
- `Karaoke Night` overlaps the existing music-night territory of *Saturday's
  Gig*.
- `Showing a Friend Around Tokyo` would require too many unrelated locations
  to earn its language gains.
- `First Day at a New Job`, `The Job Interview`, and *Presentation* compete for
  one professional slot; *Presentation* adds the most new interaction patterns.
- `Picking Up a Secondhand Sofa` is memorable but narrower than the bicycle
  story. `The Dog Without a Collar` has a strong emotional arc but lower
  near-term sentence utility for most learners.

## Completion record

The user asked for the ten packs to be completed without intermediate
checkpoints. The detached audit found and corrected four small naturalness or
register issues and one overlong learner line, then revalidated the entire
slate. Final totals are 40 stages, 204 dialogue lines, 83 learner targets, 83
accepted-variant entries, 40 narrator transitions, and 244 manifest lines.

This closes authoring only. Media generation, human listening, visual
production, encoding under `app/`, browser QA, and deployment remain separate
future actions requiring an explicit production pass.
